# MWG - simplified credit memo

**Borrower:** Mobile World Investment Corporation (HOSE: MWG)  
**Purpose of review:** educational working-capital credit case  
**Period reviewed:** FY2023-FY2025 consolidated figures

## Credit view

MWG's credit profile improved materially from 2023 to 2025. Revenue recovered from VND 118.3tn in 2023 to VND 155.9tn in 2025, while net profit increased from VND 0.17tn to VND 7.07tn. The recovery matters because 2023 shows how quickly earnings can compress in retail even when the company remains large and liquid.

Liquidity is acceptable. The current ratio was about 1.5x in 2025 and the quick ratio was close to 1.0x. More importantly, cash and short-term investments together were roughly VND 38.9tn versus around VND 29.9tn of interest-bearing debt, leaving the company in a net-cash position on this simplified definition.

Operating cash flow stayed positive at about VND 6.1tn in 2025. This was below 2024 but still covered annual interest expense several times. Debt to equity also declined from roughly 1.09x in 2023 to 0.90x in 2025.

## Main risks

1. **Inventory and working capital.** Inventory rose to about VND 27.3tn in 2025. Retail inventory can lose value quickly when product cycles or consumer demand change.
2. **Low-margin business.** Even after recovery, net margin remained below 5%, so a relatively small change in gross margin or operating costs can have a large effect on profit.
3. **Short-term funding.** Most reported borrowings are short term. This is normal for working-capital financing but increases refinancing dependence.
4. **Expansion execution.** New formats and overseas expansion can consume cash before they become profitable.

## Illustrative facility structure

For a training case, I would start with a **VND 1,500bn, 12-month revolving working-capital facility** rather than a long-dated term loan. The stated use would be seasonal inventory and supplier payments. Repayment would come from operating cash flow and inventory conversion.

I would not rely on collateral as the primary reason to lend. The first questions would be whether operating performance remains profitable, liquidity stays adequate, and short-term debt does not grow much faster than sales or equity.

### Simple monitoring points

- current ratio > 1.2x
- total debt / equity < 1.5x
- positive annual operating cash flow
- no sharp deterioration in inventory relative to revenue
- review if cash + short-term investments fall below total interest-bearing debt

## Conclusion

On the figures reviewed, MWG looks capable of supporting a moderate short-term working-capital line. The main positive factors are improving profitability, positive operating cash flow and strong liquid financial assets. The main watch items are inventory, short-term borrowing and the sensitivity of retail margins to demand and execution.

This memo is an educational case study based only on public information. It is not a bank credit approval or investment recommendation.
